apricate /ˈap-rə-ˌkāt/ verb
To bask in the sun. Which is exactly what you'll be doing once summer's sorted. (ah-pree) is a new way to coordinate your summer childcare plans with your kids' best friends.

Whether you are a working parent or full-time parent, you already have a full-time job. Planning for summer shouldn't become your night shift.

Every January, it starts: the spreadsheet, the 7am registration alarm, the camp lotteries, the group text that hits 46 messages before lunch. Somebody in your friend group becomes the unpaid summer coordinator. It's probably you.

apri visualizes everyone's plans for each week in one private space, books your camps the second registration opens, and surfaces gaps with solutions.

The kids get their friends. You get your evenings back.
